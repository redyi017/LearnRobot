# English log

Corrections from our chats. They are reviewed every 2–3 weeks in an English review session.

## Recurring patterns (updated at each review)
- **Articles (a/an/the) missing:** "I had ~~math~~ **a** math background", "~~clear definition~~ **a clear definition**"
- **Singular/plural after quantity words:** "many ~~company~~ **companies**", "~~are not robot~~ **are not robots**"
- **consider X (to be) Y**, without *as*: "consider ~~screwdriver as robot~~ **a screwdriver a robot**"
- **Verb form after auxiliaries** (would/should/do): "I would ~~to discuss~~ **like to discuss**", "should be put … or ~~being~~ **be** reviewed"
